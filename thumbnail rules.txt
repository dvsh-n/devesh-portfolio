Publication video thumbnails

Keep the source videos unchanged. Generate separate thumbnail videos with
the matching scripts/build-*-thumbnail.py script, then point the publication
media entry in src/data/publications.ts to the generated thumbnail.
Rebuild the thumbnail whenever its source video changes.

Background treatment (baked into the generated video):
- Scale to cover 640 x 400 and crop to that size.
- Gaussian blur: gblur=sigma=55:steps=3.
- Brightness: -0.12.
- Saturation: 0.6.
- RGB multipliers: rr=0.55:gg=0.55:bb=0.55.
- Enlarge the blurred background to fill the final 8:5 frame.
- Scale the sharp foreground to the largest size that fits the final frame,
  preserving its aspect ratio (force_original_aspect_ratio=decrease).
- Center it over the blurred background. Only unused letterbox/pillarbox
  areas should show blur. Never leave the foreground at native size in a
  larger frame, and never crop or stretch it to fill the frame.
- No audio; H.264/yuv420p with faststart for browser playback.
